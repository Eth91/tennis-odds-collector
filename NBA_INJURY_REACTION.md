**Both are NO-GO.** By the rule fixed in advance, this data doesn't support building a stale-line flagger as designed.
Two things in the data still matter, and both are written up below as exploratory, not as passes:

- The report clearly moves teammates' lines, but over the first hour, not in the first minutes. The close-based
  control test dilutes that, because by the close the control props have drifted too.
- The DK-LAG value measure was blind to half the lags. It priced DraftKings against a FanDuel rung at DraftKings'
  line, and FanDuel almost never offers a two-sided rung away from its main line. So every lag with a line gap went
  unmeasured, and those are the bigger ones.

## 2. Line movement, baseline to close

- **Baseline:** the book's last check at or before the report, no more than 30 min before it.
- **Close:** the last check before tip, no more than 30 min before it.
- **Moved:** a different main line, or 3.0+ no-vig points on the same line.
- **Control:** props of teams playing that day with no fresh Out or Doubtful in that report or the previous 12, over
  the same report-to-close window.

| Set | Props | Moved | Pulled | Unchanged | Moved up | Median line move | Line changed | Median no-vig move (line same) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teammates, FanDuel | 1,287 | 43.3% | 59 | 671 | 65.5% | 1.0 | 72% | 4.3 pts |
| Teammates, DraftKings | 1,160 | 45.8% | 57 | 572 | 58.4% | 1.0 | 75% | 4.3 pts |
| Control, FanDuel | 15,706 | 29.4% | 613 | 10,471 | 44.9% | 1.0 | 69% | 4.2 pts |
| Control, DraftKings | 14,248 | 31.3% | 398 | 9,397 | 40.8% | 1.0 | 78% | 4.0 pts |

Teammates move more often than the control, and upward more often (65% vs 45% on FanDuel). But at the close the gap is
1.47×, not 2×. More than half the teammate props never moved at all.

## 3. Repricing latency after the report (movers)
- **Latency** is an interval [L, U]:
  - U is the first check that isn't stale;
  - L is the last stale check before it, or the report time if there wasn't one.
- **Moved by t** is the share of eventual movers with U ≤ t.

| Book | Median U | Median L | Moved by 1 min | 5 min | 15 min | 60 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FanDuel teammates | 15.3 min | 5.9 min | 5.0% | 30.2% | 49.9% | 75.9% |
| DraftKings teammates | 20.9 min | 0.0 (bound only) | 0.0% | 20.2% | 49.3% | 79.8% |
| FanDuel control | 107.7 min | 98.9 min | 0.8% | 2.4% | 7.9% | 30.0% |
| DraftKings control | 83.9 min | 74.9 min | 0.0% | 1.5% | 9.4% | 38.4% |

**When there was no news before the report,** FanDuel was fast:
- the median U was 5.8 min;
- 44% of eventual movers had moved by 5 min and 64% by 15.

**When an X or Bluesky item came first,** later moves were slower (median U 29.9 min) and closer to background drift.
The book had likely already moved before the report.

- **Confirmed stale** means a stale check at or after report + t.
- **Unknown** means no check between the report + t and the move.

| Book | Stale at 1 min (unknown) | Stale at 5 min (unknown) | Stale at 15 min (unknown) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FanDuel | 77.9% (95) | 54.0% (88) | 43.8% (35) |
| DraftKings | 41.6% (310) | 39.5% (214) | 34.5% (86) |
| Both | 60.2% (405) | 47.0% (302) | 39.2% (121) |

DraftKings' unknowns are large because its first check after a report came a median 14.9 min later.

**First change after the report:**
- FanDuel: 689 moved first, 144 pulled first (props taken down, often reposted), 494 never changed.
- DraftKings: 592 moved first, 262 pulled first, 306 never changed.

## 5. DraftKings lagging FanDuel, and the reverse
For a prop where both books had a baseline and the leader moved by the close: was the other book still stale at its
first check after the leader's first move?

| Direction | Leader movers | Lag | No lag | Unknown | Lag rate | Median line gap | Median leader-fair EV (measurable; not) | Median minutes after the leader moved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DraftKings lags FanDuel | 455 | 175 | 274 | 6 | 38.5% | 1.0 | −5.7% (87; 88) | 2.9 |
| FanDuel lags DraftKings | 511 | 253 | 233 | 25 | 49.5% | 0.0 | −4.4% (95; 158) | 1.0 |

- **Line gap when DraftKings lagged:**
  - 0 points in 87 cases (FanDuel only moved its price);
  - 1 point in 87;
  - 2 points in 1.
- **The measurable EV covers only the 0-point cases.** FanDuel has a two-sided rung away from its main line in about
  1% of states, so a 1-point gap can't be priced against FanDuel. A 3–5 point price move doesn't beat DraftKings' vig,
  so −5.7% is expected for those cases. It says nothing about the line-gap cases.
- **FanDuel isn't uniformly first.** In half the props DraftKings moved first, FanDuel was still stale at its next
  check, mostly on price, not line.

## Exploratory findings (not pre-registered; for designing Step 2, not evidence of an edge)

**A. The reaction is concentrated in the first hour.** Share of all teammate or control props that changed within t of
the report:

| Set | 5 min | 15 min | 60 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teammates, FanDuel | 18.3% | 32.2% | 48.3% |
| Control, FanDuel | 1.2% | 3.7% | 13.7% |
| Teammates, DraftKings | 15.3% | 35.6% | 56.8% |
| Control, DraftKings | 1.0% | 5.1% | 19.7% |

**B. FanDuel's stale teammate overs gained line value on FanDuel's own close.**
- **The bet:** a teammate prop still unchanged at FanDuel's first check after the report (a median 0.7 min after), on
  the over at that price and line.
- **Points-type markets** (points, PRA, P+R, P+A):
  - the close line was better 31.7% of the time and worse 16.0%;
  - mean line value +0.24 points, 95% game-clustered interval +0.07 to +0.46;
  - 564 bets, 28 games.
- **Control overs:** 14.9% better, 17.5% worse.
- **When the close kept the bet line,** the price CLV was −5.3%, which is the vig.
- **What isn't known:** whether the line value outweighs the vig. That needs a line-to-probability conversion this
  data doesn't have.
- **DraftKings** shows no such effect (−0.05, interval −0.12 to +0.03), because its first check comes too late.

**C. DraftKings' stale line ended behind DraftKings' own close.**
- **The bet:** the 175 DraftKings lags, on the side FanDuel moved toward.
- **Against DraftKings' close:**
  - the close line was better 35.4% of the time and worse 4.0%;
  - mean line value +0.37, interval +0.24 to +0.47;
  - 26 games.
- **In the 88 lags with a line gap:** better 53.4%, worse 3.4%, mean +0.56 points.
- **The lag lasted** a median of about 20 min until DraftKings' next change. That's coarse: it's measured at
  DraftKings' check times.
- **Same direction as separate evidence:** the nightly side-by-side DK_LAGS_FD rule (+3.6% CLV on 997). That's a
  different measurement, not a validation.

## Missing data, limitations and confounders
- **Small sample.** 42 events with lines, 32 games, 9 dates, 126 teammates. Units within an event are correlated
  (one Out moves several teammates and markets together), so prop counts overstate the information. The intervals
  above resample games.
- **DraftKings' timing is coarse.** Its snapshot is 3–9+ min apart and lands about 4 s before each report. DraftKings'
  latency and its stale-at-t figures are bounds with many unknowns.
- **The next day's games are invisible.** 19 of 61 events had no props posted yet, so reports the evening before a
  game can't be studied.
- **Main line only.** States with more than one two-sided rung are rare (about 1–2%), so main-line flips aren't a
  material artifact. All 803 line-change movers moved to a line that wasn't two-sided at the baseline.
- **Pulls.** A prop pulled at the close is counted as pulled, not moved. Partial fetches replace the board without
  logging pulls, which can leave a phantom rung until the next full fetch.
- **Earlier news.** 21 events had an X, Bluesky or Rotowire item first, and other sources (beat writers, team
  announcements we don't track) may have broken more. A "fresh" official Out isn't always news to the books. That shows
  up as the events with no reaction for hours.
- **"Prior not listed"** includes the first report that lists a game, so an injury from the previous game may already
  have been known.
- **Other confounders:**
  - several Outs on one team (9 events);
  - betting-driven moves unrelated to the injury (the control measures their rate, not which teammate move they
    explain);
  - opponents' props (not analyzed);
  - FanDuel's refresh is triggered by our own watcher, so FanDuel's measured cadence is better around reports than
    in general.
- **The pre-registered DK-LAG value measure** can't price line gaps. That's a design flaw I missed when writing the
  pre-registration. The verdict stands as written, but it's uninformative for the line-gap half of the lags.

## Pass/fail for future validation (fixed in the pre-registration, unchanged)
- **Data:** forward only, after the 2026-11-03 09:56 UTC backup.
- **The rule:** written as an amendment before any of that data is examined.
- **Sample:** 200+ qualifying flags across 10+ dates, one per (event, player, market, book), at the price available at
  flag time.
- **Pass needs both:**
  - primary: mean CLV > 0 with the 95% game-bootstrap low end > 0, against the same book's no-vig close;
  - secondary: beats a same-time random-prop null by 3.0+ points.
- **Fail:** an ROI whose 95% interval sits wholly below 0 fails on its own.
- **Nothing goes live without you.**

## Recommended next step
**Step 2a, read-only on the Mac. Price the line value, then decide whether either exploratory candidate is worth a
forward test.**

1. **Pre-register an amendment first:** the conversion method, the two candidate rules, and their go/no-go.
2. **Build a line-to-probability conversion:** how many no-vig points one line point is worth, by market and line
   level. Source: the 2023-25 four-book history's alt rungs and FanDuel/DraftKings' one-sided ladders.
3. **Re-score findings B and C with full CLV** (price plus line value) on this same backup. That's in-sample, so it
   can only rule a candidate out, not in.
   - **Candidate C:** DraftKings still stale after FanDuel moved its line, on FanDuel's side. It has the stronger
     signal and matches the nightly side-by-side.
   - **Candidate B:** FanDuel teammate overs on points-type markets, still stale at FanDuel's first check after the
     report.
4. **Any survivor gets the forward 200-flag test above,** shadow only.

One data change would make DraftKings measurable: a DraftKings snapshot 1, 3 and 5 min after each report.
- It's the Mac push cron, so it needs your explicit go-ahead and a reviewed plan. I haven't changed it.
- Without it, DraftKings latency stays a bound of 9+ min before about 3 pm CST.
